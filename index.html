How to play

Nine gestures, one sky

  • ✋
    Open palm — a bloom of fireworks keeps bursting where your hand is. Both palms open sets off the grand finale.
  • ☝️
    Point — your fingertip becomes a sparkler. Move it fast to throw a streak of fire.
  • 🤏
    Pinch — grab a shell out of the air and let go to fling it up where it bursts. Pinch near a diya to light it.
  • ✌️
    Peace sign — spins a chakri at your fingertips, trailing golden sparks.
  • 👉
    Finger gun — thumb and index in an L shape launch a three-rocket volley.
  • 🤘
    Horns — a rising fountain of sparks pours out of your hand.
  • 👌
    OK sign — draws a rangoli: rings of coloured sparks blooming outward.
  • ✊
    Fist — hold to charge; open your hand to launch a huge shell. The longer you hold, the bigger the boom.
  • 👍
    Thumbs up — lights every diya in the row at once. When all eleven glow, the sky wishes everyone a Happy Diwali.

No camera? Move the mouse to draw sparklers, click for a burst, 1–9 for the gestures, Space for the finale, M to mute.
